## 1. Executive summary

### What the application is meant to do

Take a TikTok slideshow advert that already works and produce a **new**
advert that sells the same product using the same strategy — preserving
product identity and marketing argument while being visually original.

### Stage reached

Analysis is deep and largely correct. Generation runs end to end. **The join
between them is broken**, and I broke part of it today.

### What works reliably (verified)

| | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Import from TikTok, incl. share links and scheme-less URLs | `a93d87f`, 26 tests, live import of 8 slides |
| OCR text capture | Slideshow `c9c68661` slide 0: captured `£67` and "Bro let me put you on…" verbatim |
| Slideshow-level narrative beats | `cc1476d5`: `hook`/`story`×6/`cta` correct on all 8 slides |
| Provider routing / retry / failover separation | `4bad92f`, `173edc1`; variance run: 8/8 on the intended model, 0 retries, 0 fallbacks |
| Cost instrumentation | `provider_calls` populated; unpriced work fails closed |
| Benchmark governance | VERSION 4, CHANGELOG, comparability regression test |

### What does not work reliably

- **Copy preservation for screenshot-style creatives** — total loss. My defect.
- **Product identity when the product import fails** — silently proceeds with a phantom product.
- **Narrative reaching generation** — computed, persisted, never consumed by the prompt builder.
- **Image quality** — 1 in 8 ship-ready, measured (§ `docs/variance/CASE02_RESULTS.md`).
- **Validation** — accepted a blank chat mockup at 0.868 confidence.

### Most serious regressions (all introduced today)

1. **`f9e3348`** — `_overlay_text` filters OCR `surface == "overlay"`. For any
   creative whose text lives *inside* the photographed scene (chats, DMs,
   notes screens), this returns `[]`, which then emits an explicit prompt
   instruction *"the original creative has NO overlay WORDS"*. The advert is
   actively suppressed. **This produced the blank chat bubbles.**
2. **`d613a2a`** — the TikTok buy-box CTA instruction is appended to **every**
   compiled prompt unconditionally, including slides that must not have a CTA.
3. **`8ebe4c6`** — pointer-emoji stripping. Correct in intent; interacts with
   (1) and remains unvalidated for screenshot creatives.

### Is the output commercially usable?

**No.** Measured at 12.5% ship-ready on the best-understood benchmark case,
and **0% under a stricter rule** that rejects fabricated platform UI (§11).
The two live slideshows the owner ran produced output he described as
commercially unusable, and I agree with that assessment.

### Distance from the intended product

**Further than the test count suggests.** The architecture is genuinely
sophisticated and the analysis genuinely understands the adverts — the stored
`arc_summary` for the haul slideshow correctly identifies the lash serum as
"the main promotional push". What is missing is the layer that turns
understanding into constraints on output. That layer does not exist. It is not
a tuning problem.

## 7. Forensic review of the two failed slideshows

Full reports: [`forensics/TECH_BUNDLE_FAILURE.md`](forensics/TECH_BUNDLE_FAILURE.md),
[`forensics/HAUL_SLIDESHOW_READING.md`](forensics/HAUL_SLIDESHOW_READING.md).

### A. Tech-accessory conversation slideshow — `c9c68661`

**What OCR captured** — everything, verbatim:

```
Bro I've been meaning to get some new tech accessories but they all
cost at least £67 😭😒          role=headline    surface=physical
Bro let me put you on...       role=subheadline surface=physical
```

**All 8 blocks `surface=physical`.** OCR followed its own prompt correctly —
it defines `physical` as including *"a screen"*, and a chat screenshot is text
on a screen. Slide 1 by contrast returned `overlay: 8, physical: 5`.

**What analysis understood** — correctly:

> `subject`: "A simulated private chat conversation used as a hook, centered
> on a relatable frustration about high tech-related costs and a friend
> teasing an insider solution, with the product or solution intentionally
> hidden."

`NarrativeStructure`: slide 0 = `hook`, slide 1 = `reveal`, arc summary names
the price comparison.

**What the transformation plan retained**: `text_overlays: []`.

**What the final prompt contained** — four instructions, all suppressing text:

1. no copy compiled (`text_overlays` empty)
2. `things_to_avoid`: *"Do not copy Snapchat branding, exact UI, usernames,
   wording, avatar, or **message content** from the original"*
3. `suppress_overlay_text` (because `text_strategy = reuse_original`):
   *"FINAL AND ABSOLUTE INSTRUCTION - TEXT: render NO text anywhere in this
   image… treat that as describing an EMPTY LAYOUT ZONE… All real copy is
   composited by the app afterwards, not by you."*
4. the composition description, by then able to describe only shapes
5. **plus** the unconditional `PLATFORM LAYOUT` buy-box paragraph — the
   invented pointing hand

**Where meaning disappeared**: `_overlay_text` → `[]` → `no_overlays` prompt
branch. Then never restored, because the app that was supposed to composite
the copy — the typography renderer — **is disabled** (fonts not installed).
The blank bubbles are the promised empty layout zones that nothing filled.

**Why validation permitted it**: identity validation ran 0 times (skipped,
correctly — no product). Quality assessment `accepted=1`, confidence
**0.868**, with `creative_fidelity_json` and `text_quality_json` both
**empty**. The story path accepts on photorealism alone.

**This is an analysis-and-planning failure, not generation variance.**

### B. £20 ranking slideshow — `cc1476d5`

**Was it analysed as a group?** Yes — `NarrativeStructure` covered all 8
slides and produced an accurate arc:

> "…ends by spotlighting a lash growth serum with urgency around a sale,
> functioning as the main promotional push."

**Was each slide analysed independently?** Yes, and that analysis is what
reaches generation. The group analysis does not.

**Did it know different products were intentionally present?** **No.**

```
slide 0 | (none)
slide 1 | Security Check | primary
slide 2 | Security Check | primary
  … through …
slide 7 | Security Check | primary
```

**"Security Check" is TikTok's bot-block page.** The product importer hit the

16 images were generated against one.

**Did it know which product was promoted?** The narrative artefact did (the
lash serum). The product layer did not — it assigned one phantom product to
all seven slides at `prominence=primary`.

**Was Product Lock applied globally?** Effectively yes — every slide 1-7 got
the same lock profile, because every slide got the same product.

**Why did slide 0 stay close while the rest collapsed?** *Hypothesis, not
verified*: slide 0 has **no product appearance**, so it took the story path
(`run_story_generation_attempt`) which conditions on the slide's own source
image and does no product substitution. Slides 1-7 took the product path with
a lock profile pointing at a phantom. **This should be confirmed by reading
`prompt_used` for slides 0 and 4 before acting on it.**

**Why did validation accept them?** Same as §7A — the checks that exist do not
ask whether the right product is present or whether the narrative survived.

## 8. Known regressions from the last 48 hours

Ranked by likelihood of causing the observed failures.

### R1 — `f9e3348` "Overlay copy comes from OCR" — **VERY HIGH**

- **Change**: `_overlay_text` gates spec copy on `surface == "overlay"`.
- **Why it causes the failures**: screenshot-style creatives have zero
  `overlay` blocks; the `no_overlays` branch then explicitly instructs the
  model that the original has no text.
- **Tested?** **Yes — confirmed against stored artefacts.** Slide 0 of
  `c9c68661`: `_overlay_text` returns `[]`, spec has `text_overlays: []`.
- **Recommendation**: **revert-test first.** Reverting restores the older
  "invents copy" behaviour, which is bad but visible. Better: keep the
  inventory idea, change the gate from `surface` to OCR `role`.

### R2 — `d613a2a` "The buy box is bottom left" — **HIGH**

- **Change**: `placement_instruction()` appended to **every** compiled prompt.
- **Why**: injects a CTA affordance into slides that must not have one.
- **Tested?** **Yes** — the paragraph is present verbatim in slide 0's stored
  `prompt_used`, and the invented hand appears in the output.
- **Recommendation**: **review, not revert.** Condition on the slide having a
  CTA. The rule itself is correct.

### R3 — `8ebe4c6` "A pointer emoji is an affordance" — **MEDIUM**

- **Change**: strips directional emoji from overlay copy.

## 13. Owner-specified intended direction

**Recorded as requirement, not as implemented.** None of this exists.

```
Slideshow-level narrative analysis
              ↓
Detailed per-slide analysis
              ↓
Combined transformation specification     ← THE MISSING LAYER
              ↓
Pre-generation semantic validation        ← does not exist
              ↓
Image generation
              ↓
Post-generation semantic + visual validation  ← partially exists, too weak
```

**Stage one — slideshow-level.** Analyse all slides together for: overall
narrative; marketing strategy; creative format; hook; progression; reveal;
payoff; CTA; the promoted product; the role of incidental products; the
relationship between slides; repeated visual language; information withheld
until later; elements that must stay consistent.

**Stage two — per-slide, in detail.** Every visible object; exact text; text
position; typography; interface elements; platform overlays; simulated
interface elements; product identity; product role; background; lighting;
framing; composition; visual hierarchy; slide-specific purpose; relationship
to the slideshow narrative.

**The combination step** must decide, per slide: what must remain; what may
change; what *should* change for originality; what must not be introduced;
how the slide fulfils its role in the story; how structurally similar the
result should be; how visually different; whether the promoted product
appears on that slide at all; whether non-promoted products must stay
distinct; and the exact text or meaning to preserve.

The owner's worked example, which the current system cannot produce:

> Slideshow analysis: *"This slide is number four in a ranking sequence and
> must display a different incidental product."*
> Slide analysis: *"A packaged Spectrum A10 blusher brush, centred on a
> wooden surface, ranking text at the top."*
> Transformation planner: *"Preserve the rank-four role, one-product
> composition and ranking style. The incidental brush can be replaced with
> another plausible low-cost beauty item, but **not** with the promoted lash
> serum, because that would destroy the ranking narrative."*

**Pre-generation** the specification should be checked for semantic fidelity
to the original. **Post-generation** the result should be checked for
narrative role, marketing meaning, product correctness, text correctness,
slide-to-slide continuity, sufficient originality, absence of copied platform
UI, absence of invented elements, and expected structural similarity.
